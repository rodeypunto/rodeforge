1. Controller and contact

This policy explains how data is processed when you use Bombait, an Android game played locally on a shared phone, including Duel mode against another person or a bot.

The data controller is Carlos Torres, established in Spain, who develops the game under the RodeForge Interactive brand.

For privacy enquiries, help or requests relating to your data, email rodeatope@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your phone

Bombait does not require an account, email address, public chat or connection to play. Names you enter, avatars you choose and your preferences are stored on the phone to set up matches and remember your choices. Participant names are not included in the usage events the game sends to the analytics provider.

You can edit participants or turn off “Recordar participantes” (remember participants). You can also delete local data using the app’s clear storage option in Android settings. This resets preferences and does not delete data already received by advertising or analytics providers.

When sharing a phone, other people with access to the device may see the information stored on it.

3. Product analytics

We use a product analytics service to understand how Bombait is used and improve the experience. Statistics include app launches and gameplay sessions, modes used, participant counts, rounds, results and activity relating to ads.

The service also processes technical data, such as installation and session identifiers, device and operating system information, app and service versions, and activity dates and times. Connections communicate an IP address, which the provider may use for approximate location and security. Certain data, such as advertising identifiers, depends on available choices and the settings applicable to the service.

This data may link activity from the same installation even without names. It is therefore not considered completely anonymous. On a shared phone, installation activity does not necessarily represent a single person. Some events may be sent when a connection becomes available again.

4. Advertising and your choices

Bombait uses an advertising service to display full-screen ads after matches. The service may automatically collect and share IP addresses, general location estimated from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers where applicable, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

The advertising provider uses this data to deliver and measure advertising, maintain its services and prevent fraud. Actual processing depends, among other factors, on the device and applicable privacy choices.

Advertising privacy choices are managed through the advertising provider’s form. You can reopen the available choices through Ajustes → Privacidad de anuncios (Settings → Advertising privacy). You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings. These actions do not automatically erase previous information or disable product analytics.

5. Purposes and privacy choices

Local data enables match setup and personalization. Analytics helps us understand game usage and results; advertising helps fund the game. If you email the contact address, we use the information in your message to reply and handle your request.

Advertising choices and product analytics are independent. Usage statistics are currently collected automatically, and Bombait does not provide a visible switch to disable them. Advertising choices are not presented as consent to analytics.

6. Providers and recipients

The advertising and analytics providers receive the data needed to provide their respective services. Their infrastructure providers, subprocessors and other recipients may also be involved under the applicable terms and policies. The analytics provider acts as a data processor for game analytics.

You can learn more about how they use data in the official policies linked at the end of this page.

7. Data retention

Local data remains on the device until replaced or deleted. Support emails are deleted one year after the enquiry is closed.

Providers apply retention terms that vary by data, purpose and service. There is no single period common to all information. Uninstalling Bombait or clearing its local storage does not automatically delete data already transmitted to the advertising or analytics providers.

8. Help, deletion and rights

For help with your data or to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection where applicable, email rodeatope@gmail.com. Where processing relies on your consent, you may request its withdrawal. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your competent authority.

Bombait has no accounts linking your email to installation data. This limitation may affect our ability to locate records relating to your request. Immediate deletion is not guaranteed. Avoid sending identity documents or other sensitive data without a justified need.

9. Children and shared use

Bombait is intended for teenagers and adults. A content rating does not constitute authorization to process personal data.

When a phone is shared, choices made on it do not establish the age or authorization of everyone playing. Parents and guardians may email rodeatope@gmail.com with enquiries about children’s privacy.

10. International transfers and security

The advertising and analytics providers may process data outside Spain and the European Economic Area. Their documentation describes international transfer mechanisms, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses, as applicable.

The providers document safeguards such as encryption during transmission; the analytics provider also describes encryption of stored data. No system guarantees absolute security. Local data protection also depends on phone security and who can access the device.

11. This page and policy changes

These pages do not include cookies, forms, analytics or advertising. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives technical data needed to serve it, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement. Provider links do not load their services inside this page.

This policy will be reviewed when Bombait’s practices change. The date at the beginning shows when the document was last updated.
